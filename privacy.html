3. 제3자 제공

운영자는 이용자의 개인정보를 제3자에게 팔거나 제공하지 않습니다. 다만 법령에 따라 수사기관 등이 정해진 절차로 요구하는 경우는 예외입니다.

4. 만 14세 미만 아동

게임은 만 13세 이상을 대상으로 합니다. 온라인 기능이 들어간 뒤에는 처음 실행할 때 출생 연도를 확인하고, 만 14세 미만 이용자에게는 법정대리인의 동의 없이 채팅 · 맞춤형 광고 · 이용 통계 수집을 켜지 않습니다. 만 14세 미만 아동의 개인정보가 동의 없이 수집된 사실을 알게 되면 바로 지웁니다.

5. 파기 절차와 방법

보유 기간이 끝나거나 삭제 요청을 받으면 지체 없이(5일 안에) 파기합니다. 전자 파일은 되살릴 수 없는 방법으로 지웁니다. 법령에 따라 보관해야 하는 정보(구매 기록 등)는 다른 정보와 분리해 두었다가 그 기간이 끝나면 파기합니다.

6. 이용자와 법정대리인의 권리

이용자(만 14세 미만은 법정대리인)는 언제든 자신의 개인정보를 열람 · 정정 · 삭제하거나 처리를 멈추도록 요구할 수 있습니다. 게임 설정이나 이메일(yosec5021@gmail.com)로 요청하면 10일 안에 조치하고 결과를 알립니다. 계정과 데이터를 지우는 방법은 계정 · 데이터 삭제에 있습니다.

7. 안전성 확보 조치

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Operator YOSEC · Contact yosec5021@gmail.com

YOSEC (“we”) protects the personal information of people who play the mobile game “Last Train Run: Zombie Defense” (“the game”) under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

At a glance
  • The game never asks for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • In the current version all game data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to other companies’ servers.
  • Online features (cloud save, leaderboards, chat, rewarded ads, in-app purchases) will process the data in 1-3 only from the version that adds them. We will update this policy and tell you in the game before that.
  • We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we process

1-1. Kept only on your device

Your progress (stages, survivors, gear, currencies, settings, tutorial), the nickname used for leaderboards, whether you accepted the chat rules, your block list and reports not yet sent are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset data removes them.

1-2. When you e-mail us

Your e-mail address, your message and anything you include (such as your in-game ID), used to answer you and handle requests such as account deletion. Kept for 1 year after we reply, or 3 years for payment-related inquiries (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).

2. Other companies and transfers abroad

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services) runs our game servers on our behalf, in the United States and other countries where Unity has data centers. Google LLC (United States) provides e-mail (Gmail), AdMob ads, Google Play Billing and Play Games, and processes data under its own policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections only when you use those features. See the Unity Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. If you do not use cloud save, leaderboards, chat, ads or purchases, that data is not transferred and you can keep playing on your device.

3. Third parties

We do not sell or give your personal information to third parties, except when a public authority requests it through a procedure required by law.

4. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. Once online features are added, we will ask for your birth year at first launch and will not turn on chat, personalized ads or usage statistics for players under 14 without a parent’s consent. If we learn that we collected data from a child under 14 without consent, we delete it right away.

5. Deletion

When the retention period ends or you ask us to delete your data, we delete it without delay (within 5 days) so that it cannot be recovered. Data we must keep by law (such as purchase records) is stored separately and deleted when that period ends.

6. Your rights

You (or a parent for a child under 14) may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop processing it, through Settings or by e-mail (yosec5021@gmail.com). We act within 10 days. See Delete account & data.

7. Security

All traffic between the game and servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Only the operator can access personal data, and the accounts we use for operation have two-step verification turned on. Secret keys such as those used to verify purchases are kept only in the server’s secret store.

8. Cookies and identifiers

The game uses no cookies. Once ads are added, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names differ by device). This website uses no cookies or analytics; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process access logs such as IP addresses (GitHub General Privacy Statement).
