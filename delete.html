한국어

Delete account & data

Last Train Run: Zombie Defense (달려라 막차: 좀비 디펜스) · Developer YOSEC · Package com.yosec.lasttrainrun

Current version

All game data is stored only on your device and nothing is kept on a server. Option 1 below removes everything.

Option 1 · In the game

  1. Tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  2. On the “Game” tab, tap Reset data, then tap again to confirm.
  3. All progress, currencies, gear and settings on this device are deleted and the game starts over.

Uninstalling the app also removes the game data stored on the device. From the version that adds online features (cloud save, leaderboards, chat), the same settings screen will also let you request deletion of your server account and records.

Option 2 · By e-mail, without the app

Send an e-mail to yosec5021@gmail.com with the subject “Last Train Run account deletion” and your in-game ID (Settings → Support → My ID), or your nickname and the date you last played. We delete the data within 7 days and reply.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: game account ID, nickname, cloud save, leaderboard records, mail, chat messages, crash and usage statistics.
Kept: purchase records (order number, item, time) for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce consumer protection law; report handling records for 1 year; e-mail inquiries for 1 year after we reply (3 years if payment-related). Google keeps its own Google Play payment records under its policy. Deleted game data cannot be recovered.
